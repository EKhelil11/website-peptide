## Batch-Level Verification Standard

All 16 supplied reports are addressed to **LA Elite Sales LLC** and use the same Ethos Analytics Certificate of Analysis format. Contact-sheet review confirmed Ethos Analytics laboratory branding, the Phoenix laboratory address, accreditation number `117798`, the Ethos sample identifier, HPLC method, signed Laboratory Director approval, and the report limitation that results apply only to the specified sample.

Each mapped product page may therefore state only the facts shown in its own report. The website should use the wording **reported chromatographic purity >99% for lot [lot]** rather than applying a catalog-wide purity claim. Blend reports should display their combined report result without implying that each component was independently assigned its own purity percentage.

## Document and Display Boundaries

The verified product pages may display the mapped lot, report ID, received/completed/tested dates, HPLC method, reported content result, `>99%` chromatographic-purity result, laboratory identity, accreditation reference, and direct PDF download. The pages must also state that the result applies to the specific tested lot and does not establish a blanket result for other lots or products.

No report contains a public verification URL. The owner-supplied PDF is the source document for each mapped record. Filename spelling differences—particularly `SEMORELIN` versus the analyte spelling `Sermorelin`—are documented but do not prevent product matching because the report body, strength, and lot are consistent.
