## Typography

The newly approved typography remains unchanged. Cormorant Garamond is used for display headings, Rajdhani for compact labels and interface controls, and Inter for body copy, forms, checkout, account, admin, and legal text. Italics remain limited to short decorative accent phrases rather than functional copy.

## Surface Strategy

Public pages use soft ivory as the main canvas with alternating warm-limestone and champagne bands. Product cards use ivory bodies, subtle brushed-silver borders, and champagne image stages. Royal blue carries all primary actions and navigation emphasis; metallic silver-gray supports category labels, dividers, and document status.

Midnight navy is retained intentionally for the opening intro, age-verification overlay, announcement bar, selected hero contrast areas, and footer. These dark surfaces use ivory body text, silver labels, and royal-blue or champagne highlights. This prevents the site from becoming either excessively dark or stark white.

Authentication, checkout, account, admin, legal, COA, and error pages follow the same warm-light canvas, ivory cards, silver borders, charcoal body text, and royal-blue actions. Error and destructive states may retain semantic red only where meaning requires it.
