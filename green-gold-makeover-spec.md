# LA Elite Peptides Green-and-Gold Makeover Specification

## Direction

The redesign will use an original **heritage-luxury laboratory** aesthetic inspired only by the general combination of deep green and refined gold. It will not reproduce Rolex trademarks, logos, watch imagery, crown symbols, proprietary layouts, or advertising copy.

The owner-supplied silver-and-blue LA Elite Peptides logo will remain unchanged. It will be presented on deep green or near-black green surfaces with restrained gold framing so the existing artwork remains legible without being recolored.

## Typography and Hierarchy

Display headings will use **Cormorant Garamond** for a high-end editorial character. Inter will remain the body font for readability, and Rajdhani will remain limited to compact technical labels, pricing, filters, and utility navigation. The approved silver-and-blue logo remains the primary brand mark.

Gold will be used as a precision accent rather than as a full-page fill. Large body copy will remain warm ivory or charcoal depending on surface. Buttons will use deep green with gold borders or refined-gold fills with dark-green text, preserving visible focus states and active feedback.

## Surface Strategy

Public pages will alternate between deep green, forest panels, and warm ivory sections to avoid a flat all-dark treatment. Product cards will use forest-green frames with restrained gold edge lighting and warm ivory content areas where useful. Forms will use ivory or very pale sage inputs with dark-green text. Legal and account pages will use calm ivory reading surfaces framed by green navigation.

The opening intro will retain its current background image and approved logo size, but cyan and magenta light effects will shift to emerald and muted gold. The age gate will keep its behavior while adopting the same green-and-gold visual language.

## Protected Business State

The makeover will not change the 11-product catalog, Retatrutide 30 mg price of `$200`, product copy, COA availability status, cart calculations, checkout submission behavior, authentication, database schema, Resend, ShipStation, secrets, domain routing, or research-use-only safeguards.

## Publication Rule

All redesign work will remain uncheckpointed in the private development preview until the owner reviews and explicitly approves publication. The protected baseline is checkpoint `8bb38c66`.
