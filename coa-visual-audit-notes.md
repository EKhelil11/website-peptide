# COA Visual Audit Notes

## Direct Visual Confirmation

Direct page review of the GHK-Cu 100 mg and KLOW 80 mg reports confirmed that the laboratory identity appears graphically as **Ethos Analytics Laboratory**, even when PDF text extraction omits the logo and footer. The documents display the Phoenix address `3020 E Camelback Rd STE 397, Phoenix, AZ 85016`, accreditation number `117798`, laboratory contact information, a handwritten-style signature, and **Noel Samsum — Laboratory Director**.

Both directly reviewed reports use a consistent Certificate of Analysis layout with the LA Elite Sales LLC customer name, an Ethos `EA Sample ID`, sample name, powder sample type, lot/batch, received and completed dates, HPLC summary, measured content versus specification, identity confirmation by retention-time concordance with a certified reference standard under USP `<621>`, chromatographic-purity result, LOQ, and a signed completion date.

The footer expressly limits each report to the specific sample identified in that document. Accordingly, the website must associate each COA with its exact canonical product and stated lot/batch rather than presenting a catalog-wide verification statement.
