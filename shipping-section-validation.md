# Shipping Section Private-Preview Validation

## Visual Direction

The former solid blue shipping band has been replaced by a light warm-limestone and champagne gradient that connects naturally to the ivory product cards and surrounding home sections. The redesign uses Cormorant Garamond for the editorial heading, Rajdhani for utility labels, Inter for body copy, royal blue for hierarchy and actions, brushed-silver borders, warm-ivory cards, and charcoal text for strong readability.

The main experience is structured as one premium fulfillment card with four clear service points and a dedicated `3–5 Business Days` delivery panel. A champagne action strip explains that charges appear before order submission and provides a royal-blue catalog action. Four smaller reassurance cards reinforce discreet packaging, all-state coverage, the standard delivery window, and research-order positioning without returning to a dark background.

## Shipping Boundaries

The section states nationwide shipping to all 50 states and a 3–5 business-day delivery timeframe. It does not advertise same-day, overnight, 24-hour dispatch, cold-chain integrity, or cold-pack packaging. Shipping charges remain calculated at checkout, and no fulfillment, ShipStation, payment, database, or route behavior was changed.

## Validation

Desktop review confirmed the lighter section is visually uniform with the catalog, the blue and silver hierarchy is readable, the delivery panel is prominent, and the catalog action remains visible. A 375 px full-page review confirmed the section stacks without horizontal overflow and preserves the heading, feature cards, delivery promise, action strip, and reassurance items within the mobile page flow. Normal session-based age verification was restored before automated validation.

TypeScript completed without errors, all **82 active tests** passed—including the new shipping-palette and timing regression—and the production build succeeded with only the existing bundle-size advisory. No checkpoint was saved and nothing was published; the live site remains on protected baseline `8bb38c66`.
